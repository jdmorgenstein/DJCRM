# Price research — New Year's 2026–27 family trip

Researched **August 6, 2026** for travel **Dec 30, 2026 → Jan 3/4, 2027** (4–5 nights),
departing South Florida (FLL/MIA), group of ~8 adults + 3 kids across 4 family units,
budget **$3,000–4,000 per family unit**. Live holiday quotes mostly sit behind booking
engines, so figures marked *(est.)* extrapolate a 30–60% holiday premium onto published
rates. These are the baselines loaded into the planner's defaults — verify with the
pre-filled search links before booking.

Grandparent couples (2 adults) run roughly 55–65% of the family-of-4 numbers everywhere.

## Beach all-inclusive (New Year's week)

- **Punta Cana** — cheapest beach option. 3.5–4★ family AI *(est.)* $80–150/adult/night;
  kids under 12 often **free or half price** (Dreams, Sunscape, Club Med model). Value picks:
  Bahia Principe Grand/Bavaro, Riu Bambu, Vista Sol, Occidental Caribe, Grand Palladium,
  Whala Bavaro. Flights FLL→PUJ *(est.)* $250–450 budget carriers / $350–550 mainline;
  Southwest now runs ~20 weekly FLL–PUJ flights (2 free bags each).
- **Cancun / Riviera Maya** — +15–35% over Punta Cana; hard data point: 5★ family AI over
  Christmas/NY ran $1,100–1,390/night for a family of 4. Budget path is older Riviera Maya
  inventory (Riu Lupita, Viva, Seadust) + Spirit/Frontier. **Mandatory NYE gala supplements**
  (~$100/adult, ~$50/child) are common — confirm in writing.
- **Montego Bay** — family-resort tier prices at or above Cancun; value pick is Holiday Inn
  Resort Montego Bay (kids stay/eat free). Riu Montego Bay is adults-only.
- **Channels**: CheapCaribbean (transfers included, $175–250 promo codes, Black Friday
  precedent of $500 off — but NY inventory is picked over by late Nov); Costco Travel (not
  always cheapest sticker, adds transfers + shop cards); Expedia (often lowest sticker,
  nonrefundable, no transfers); JetBlue Vacations (FLL nonstops, transfers + resort credits).
  Same room can vary **$500+** across platforms. Book flights by late September.

## Drive-to-snow (from Boca Raton)

- **Banner Elk, NC** (~790 mi, 11½–12h) — Sugar Mountain's 2026–27 holiday rates are posted:
  **$99/day adult, $72 child 5–11, ≤4 $4**; Beech similar. Both 100% snowmaking → New Year's
  skiing is a near-lock. Off-site rental shops $15–25/day vs $30–40 on-mountain. Houses
  sleeping 12–14: $600–1,000/night NYE ($2,800–4,400 for 4 nights with fees).
- **Gatlinburg, TN** (~812 mi, 12½h) — Ober Mountain $89–99 peak days (small: 10 trails);
  rent downtown (on-mountain lines hit 2–3h). Huge cabin supply: sleeps-14 chalets ~$700–1,200
  NYE, early bookers ~$500–700. Natural snow odds ~1-in-4; Dollywood Smoky Mountain Christmas
  $85–95/person carries the non-ski side.
- **Snowshoe, WV** (~975 mi, 15–16h) — best Southeast skiing (60 trails, 1,500 ft vertical)
  but the drive + $129/day holiday tickets + $900–1,500/night village lodging kill the value
  for this group.
- **Gas**: ~$4.09–4.18/gal → round trip ~$250–280/car NC/TN; 3 cars ≈ $750–850.

## Fly-to-snow

- **Camelback, Poconos PA** — the sleeper. FLL→PHL $120–250 rt at peak *(est.)*;
  **Triple Ticket: 3 ski days ~$139, no blackout dates (~$199 with rentals)**; Poconos
  12-guest houses avg ~$580/night for Dec 26–Jan 2. Caveats: 800 ft vertical, crowds, thaw risk.
- **Mont-Tremblant, QC** — only near-guaranteed real winter on the list (70% of terrain open
  by Christmas, 2,116 ft vertical). Fits budget only if: budget-carrier fares booked now
  ($250–450), **advance multi-day lift tickets by Oct 15** (CAD 104 ≈ $74/day vs $127
  walk-up), modest chalet ($550–1,000/night, 5–7 night minimums). CAD ≈ $0.71 helps.
  Passports required; rental vans include Québec's mandatory winter tires.
- **Blue Mountain, ON** — ~$400–600/family cheaper than Tremblant but 720 ft vertical and
  more rain risk.
- **Vermont eliminated** — FLL→BTV $335–667 + $204–266/day holiday lift windows.
- **NC via CLT** — FLL→CLT $110–320 rt + minivan ~$350/5 days + 2h drive: saves ~9h each way
  vs driving for ~$1,000–1,600 more per family of 4.

## Odds of natural snow on the ground at the cabin (New Year's)

Slopes are white everywhere via snowmaking; these are the odds of a white yard, using
Dec 25 ≥1" climatology (NOAA 1991–2020 normals + local records) as the proxy — New Year's
runs similar or slightly better as winter deepens.

| Destination | Odds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mont-Tremblant, QC | **~90%+** | Laurentians: ~45" snow in December, 23 snowy days; Montréal alone is ~79% |
| Snowshoe, WV (4,848 ft) | **~60–75%** | Snowiest pocket of the mid-Atlantic; Dec avg 30–44" |
| Beech Mountain, NC (5,000+ ft) | **~40%** | Highest town in the eastern US; ~40% of Christmases see snowfall |
| Banner Elk valley, NC (3,700 ft) | ~20–25% | Boone (3,300 ft) measures 19% for ≥1" on the ground |
| Camelback/Poconos, PA (~2,000 ft) | ~20–30% | Real winters, but late-Dec thaws are common |
| Gatlinburg, TN (1,300 ft) | **~10–20%** | ~1" avg December snow in town; Newfound Gap (5,046 ft) nearby approaches 50% |

Practical lever in NC: booking the cabin **on Beech Mountain** instead of down in
Banner Elk/Boone roughly doubles the white-yard odds at the same trip cost.
